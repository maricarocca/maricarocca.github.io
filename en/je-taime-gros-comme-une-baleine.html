Je t'aime gros comme une baleine

A rhinoceros and a little red-billed bird live together, very close to one another, but not always at the same pace. When the rhinoceros is busy, distracted or far away, his friend feels the need to tell him how much he matters. Ant, cat, giraffe or whale: depending on the day, animals become a tender, funny way to measure love, absence and the wish to be together.
